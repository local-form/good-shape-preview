Now, what’s it swimming in?

Every shape is built to hold a certain kind of sauce. The yellow tags are the ones made for what’s in your tray. Each tub sauces two portions.

    Fresh pasta cooks in three to four minutes. Warm the sauce, save a cup of the pasta water, toss it all together.

    Four dies on the wall today.

    A die is a disc of bronze with the shape cut through it. Bronze leaves the pasta rough, and rough pasta is what sauce sticks to. We swap them through the week.

    • No. 24

      Rigatoni

      Ridges and a wide bore. Built to carry a ragù.

    • No. 31

      Fusilli

      Three fins in a tight spiral. Pesto gets caught in every turn.

    • No. 12

      Paccheri

      Big smooth tubes that fill up with sauce. Four to a forkful.

    • No. 27

      Tortiglioni

      Deep ridges that wind round the tube. They hold cream and cheese.
